Crossbound country badge artwork v2

12 countries x 3 tiers. Generated with the built-in image_gen tool.
PNG masters retain transparent alpha; web/ contains delivery-only WebP encodings.
No gameplay, reward threshold or ownership data is changed by this artwork set.

Design: a continuous shield with integrated national colors and landmark relief.
Gold / silver / bronze use 3 / 2 / 1 rank stars in a separate bottom inset.
National enamel colors remain unchanged across ranks. Rank stars are not flag stars.
US, Australia and Brazil use abstract flag-color palettes and city landmarks,
rather than miniature flags with potentially inaccurate stars or heraldic details.
These are artistic game collectibles, not official flag reproductions.
